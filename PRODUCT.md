# Product: Family Creed Generator

**User.** The family organizer: usually a parent or grandparent, sometimes a grown child or an engaged couple, who wants the family to say out loud what it stands for and end up with something worth framing. Secondary: relatives far away who answer by link, and anyone searching for family mottos, values or what heraldic symbols mean.

**Painful job.** "Turn our family's values into words and a symbol we all agree on, in one sitting, without a fight." Today the options are a blank page, a family mission-statement worksheet nobody finishes, a stranger on Etsy drawing a crest from a form, or a "family crest by surname" site that sells someone else's coat of arms.

**Promise (5 seconds).** *Pass one phone around the table. In ten minutes you have a family creed, a motto and a heraldic crest you voted on together, ready to print. Free, and nothing leaves your device.*

**The wedge.**
1. **Holiday gifting and gatherings.** The session is something to do at the table; the print is the gift. Weddings, new babies, reunions and new homes each add their own creed line and lean the crest toward the traditional design (a split shield for a marriage).
2. **Every print carries the link.** The poster's small credit line names the site, and shared links open a page whose main button is "Make one for your family". Relatives who answer by invite link see the tool too.
3. **Search.** 36 value pages, motto lists, a Latin motto guide, a heraldry guide and a weekly example answer the queries people already type: family motto ideas, Latin family mottos, family values list, what heraldry symbols mean, how to write a family creed, make your own family crest.

**Why now.** The holiday gifting window opens in November. Families gather in person. Custom-crest sellers prove the demand at $30–$150 a design, and none of them let the family make it together.

**Activation metric.** A session that ends in a download or a share (`export_*` or `share` events). Instrumented, and switched on by the GoatCounter key in OPERATIONS.md.

**First revenue event.** A family ordering a framed print or shirt of its own crest through the print-on-demand slot. It is built but off, because GitHub Pages is non-commercial; turning it on is a key in OPERATIONS.md (move hosting to Cloudflare Pages, connect a print-on-demand store).

**v1 deliberately won't:**
- call an AI at runtime, or need a server, account or upload;
- claim to find "your family's historical coat of arms";
- show who picked what (counts only);
- sell anything on GitHub Pages;
- support right-to-left scripts or non-Latin fonts in the poster (Latin and Latin Extended only).
